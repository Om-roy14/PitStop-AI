# PitStop AI — Your Career, Accelerated.

**PitStop AI** is an AI-powered career platform designed to be the one place where every aspect of your professional journey comes together. Instead of juggling resumes, portfolios, projects, job descriptions, and countless applications across different platforms, PitStop AI brings everything under one roof.

Simply paste a job posting, and PitStop AI analyzes the role, extracts the required skills and qualifications, compares them with your profile, and generates personalized, professional cold emails and job applications tailored specifically for that opportunity. Every email is powered by AI and crafted to highlight the experiences, projects, and skills that best match the role.

Beyond applications, PitStop AI acts as your personal career intelligence system. It securely organizes your resumes, projects, technical skills, certifications, work experience, portfolio links, and achievements into a unified profile that continuously evolves as you grow. The platform tracks your applications, monitors your progress, identifies skill gaps, and provides actionable insights to help you become a stronger candidate over time.

Built on an agentic AI architecture, PitStop AI automates repetitive tasks while keeping you in control. From resume parsing and job matching to application tracking, outreach automation, and career analytics, every AI agent collaborates to streamline your job search and maximize your opportunities.

**Our Vision**

To build the world's most intelligent career companion—a platform that doesn't just help people apply for jobs, but helps them grow throughout their professional journey. PitStop AI aims to become the operating system for every job seeker, empowering individuals with personalized AI assistance, smarter applications, continuous career insights, and a single destination for everything needed to build a successful career.

**One Platform. Every Opportunity. Accelerate Your Career.**
